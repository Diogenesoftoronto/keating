  1. How they were made. Sol generated 65 new sheets from the existing sprites as reference. I re-cut each one into the app's 1024 by 512 atlas with one scale per sheet, so they drop into KeatingBot as new states. Five came back with a backdrop and were regenerated.
  2. Where they are thin. Proportions drift a little between sheets: the head is slightly larger in some. A few read better as stills than as loops (lever, rope). The existing subject sprites (astronomy, biology and the rest) are not repeated here.
  3. One per event, never a queue. If two things happen at once the later one wins. Nothing plays more than once per event, and nothing plays while you are typing.

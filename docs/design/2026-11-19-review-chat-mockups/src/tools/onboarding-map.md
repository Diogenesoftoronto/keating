## Entry, container and navigation

- Chat initializes with onboarding hidden. After app storage initializes, it opens only with zero session metadata entries, no truthy `ask` or `course` query parameter, and no completed/skipped record. Storage initialization errors leave chat accessible. Chat/sidebar stay mounted under a hidden wrapper while onboarding is up. [web/src/pages/Chat.tsx:916–942,1078–1105]
- The in-flow, non-modal dialog is in a flexing outer scroll area with `min-height: 0` and `1rem` inline padding. Card width `min(100%, 560px)`, horizontal auto margins, 24px vertical margins/padding, 12px radius, 14px base copy and 26px heading. At ≤460px, 12px vertical margins, 18px padding and 23px heading. Welcome choices become two columns at ≥560px viewport. Profile fields have their own vertical scroll region, `max-height: min(56vh, 30rem)`, 20px gap and 4px right padding. Footer follows content. Panda profile rows pair fields at `sm`; groups and rows use `1rem` gaps, field stacks `0.375rem`, labels `0.75rem`. Root `font-size: 130%` makes 1rem = 20.8px at a 16px browser base. [chat-onboarding.css:1–21,31–50,58–69; profile/ProfileFieldGroups.tsx:24–47; web/panda.config.ts:1461; web/src/pages/Chat.tsx:1079]
- Header “Welcome to Keating” and always-available “Go straight to chat”; Escape inside the card does the same. Progress “{position} of {total} · {label}” and decorative done/current/ahead segments above the heading. The heading receives focus on step changes without requesting scroll. Footer “Back” appears after Welcome, “Next” advances except where explicitly hidden below, and “Skip this” appears on profile groups. All three are disabled during parent pending actions. “Back” clears parent errors, preserves the profile state and saves the destination. [ChatOnboarding.tsx:242–254,293–314,333–347,430–437]
- `localStorage` key `keating:onboarding:v1` stores v2 outcome, numeric step, and completion timestamp (null in progress). In-progress records additionally store stable step ID, access choice and track. v1/v2 completed or skipped suppress entry. Only v2 in-progress resumes, preferring the ID, otherwise clamping the numeric index. Defaults are Welcome, Keating access and form. [ChatOnboarding.tsx:31–95]
- Transitions save the current profile separately under `keating:learner-profile:v2`; editing alone updates React state. Group “Skip this” records a skipped group and still saves typed fields. Global skip or finish saves the profile and seeds a pursuit from the goal where applicable. Global skip closes without handing a goal to chat. Finish hands the trimmed goal to Chat; trimmed URL `ask` wins if present, and `ComposerPrefill` fills an empty composer once per distinct text without sending. ## Code-visible rough edges

- About you combines identity, language settings and up to eight language rows within a capped nested scroller, with Anki outside it. Your learning stuffs eight fields into the same scroller. Later fields and the outer footer may require separate scrolling. [ChatOnboarding.tsx:385–400,424; chat-onboarding.css:43–50; web/src/pages/Chat.tsx:1079]
- Keating Setup has no forward control while signed out. A failed sign-in leaves retry, Back or global skip. Progress counts Setup even when bypassed. BYOK/offline Setup can be left without a usable model; “Choose the offline model” opens the general selector. [ChatOnboarding.tsx:119–128,233–254,376–384,430–437]
- Conversation has inner “Skip”/“Skip and finish” plus outer “Skip this” and global “Go straight to chat”. “Fill a form instead” becomes “Fill the form instead” in the empty state. Ctrl/Cmd+Enter bypasses the blank guard, and extraction has no visible in-flight button lock or local catch before the last prompt returns. Skipping a question via its button also bypasses the recognizer-stop call in submit. [ProfileIntakeChat.tsx:83–104,125–144; ChatOnboarding.tsx:402–437]
- Extracted name/goal enter the profile before review even when empty-state copy says “I have kept none of it.” On conversation apply, `patch` queues the accepted values and `goTo` persists from the same render's older profile; a reload before finishing can lose accepted changes. Review has its own advance paths while outer “Next” can bypass selected verdicts. [ChatOnboarding.tsx:261–267,293–295,402–437; ProfileProposalReview.tsx:104–115]
- Accessibility helper repeats verbatim, and goal label repeats its screen heading. Number bounds are input attributes and the footer uses buttons outside form submission. [ChatOnboarding.tsx:184–195,424–436; profile/ProfileFieldGroups.tsx:404,498–520,584–587]
- The import button only advertises .apkg even though the picker accepts three text file extensions too. [AnkiInterestImport.tsx:60–72]

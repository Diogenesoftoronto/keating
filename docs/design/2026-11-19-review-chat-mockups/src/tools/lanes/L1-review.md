WHAT TO BUILD
R1 Review index
1. One compact AppBar-style page header (kit `AppBar`: back to chat labelled "Back to teaching", title "Session reviews", the privacy line as a chip with the full sentence as its title/description) and the intro sentence below it.
2. Progress as one strip: "{reviewed} of {sessions}" count, a bar that distinguishes final (solid) from in progress (striped; use a pattern, not only colour), the "{n} final · {n} in progress · {n} not reviewed" line, the problem/strength/suggestion tally with kind marks, the average rating when present, and the existing "Continue: {title}" / "Review next: {title}" action at the right. Fill the final segment with `ShaderSurface effect="ink"`.
3. Queue: filter chips with counts, search and sort on one row; each row shows a status glyph (not reviewed, in progress, final: shapes, not only colour), title, preview, "{n} turns · {model}", the date (session date, or "Reviewed {date}"), note counts by kind, and the status word with "rated {n} of 5" when present. Keep all existing states and copy for loading, error, empty, no match and the review-read error.
4. "Across your reviews" becomes a side column card (the one hard-shadowed card) at wide widths and sits under the list on narrow ones; before two eligible reviews exist it collapses to a single line. Keep its run/stop/read-again behaviour, error, results, session links and "Work on this next".
R2 to R4 Review workspace
5. Layout: a header row (kit AppBar: back to "Reviews" index; serif title; ONE status control "Draft / Final" beside the title that drives the existing review status; turns/model/date; a link back to the chat session; dirty/saving/export status text; Export; Save review) and a SubBar (Transcript / Artifacts (n) / Model results (n) tabs; "All turns / One turn" segmented control replacing "Session / Focused turn"; ONE "Rendered / Raw" switch that applies to every turn). Remove the duplicate Export and Save from the desk header and remove the separate turn-picker row.
6. Three docked columns at desktop widths: outline (turn list), page (transcript), The Margin (desk). The desk must NOT overlay the transcript; opening or collapsing it reflows the page. Outline and desk each collapse to a narrow spine. Keep the persisted collapse state.
7. Outline rows: turn number, a role square (filled for Keating/tutor, open for learner, distinct marks for tool and system turns), the first words, and marks for the kinds of note on that turn. Kinds are drawn by SHAPE and colour everywhere: problem triangle, strength octagon, suggestion diamond (see `.k.p`, `.k.s`, `.k.g` in shared.css). Collapsed spine shows the role squares with note marks.
8. Page: turns read as a document. Learner turns in the UI face, quieter; tutor turns in the reading face via kit `Prose`. Per-turn controls ("Note on this turn", overflow with "Open this turn in focused review" and anything else that exists per turn) show on the hovered/focused/active turn only, and always on touch. Saved notes are numbered in transcript order; each highlight carries its number as a small badge and the note card in the Margin carries the same number. The selection toolbar shows Problem / Strength / Suggestion with kind marks and Rewrite. A bottom hint bar shows "Select text to annotate" and the shortcuts.
9. The Margin: three tabs with counts, "Notes", "Rubric", "Rewrites", replacing the accordion (keep every panel's content and behaviour). Above them the Socratic pass as ONE button that opens a menu with its three actions, their exact existing helper or disabled-reason copy, and the model pool picker inside the menu; while a pass runs the button shows the running label with STOP, with `ShaderSurface effect="ink"` behind it. Pass errors stay dismissible.
10. Notes tab: proposals from a pass first, as dashed cards with Accept / Dismiss / "Show the turn" and "Dismiss all"; then saved notes as numbered cards (kind mark, kind, severity/category when set, quote, note; edit and delete on the current card). The note editor opens at the top of the Notes tab (not floating over the transcript at desktop widths): target and quote, kind as a three-way segmented control, "Why does this matter?", then category / severity / impact / alternative as one row of "Add" links that open the existing fields, "Finish this note" (the existing expand action), and Cancel + "Add note" / "Update" together at the bottom. The per-note Draft/Final status and the authorship tag must remain available: put them in the editor's overflow or a compact row inside the expanded details, not removed.
11. Rubric tab: each dimension shows its existing one-line hint (RUBRIC_HINTS) under the label and the chosen rating's word (ratingLabel) under the scale. A proposed scoring from a pass shows its values as dashed marks on the same scales with Apply and dismiss; applying fills them. Verdict as a segmented control, the review summary field, and the "Judgement settings" link.
12. Rewrites tab: the desk-side summary of generated alternatives for the active target (pool picker, Generate, estimate line, candidate list with state, a preview with Rendered/Raw, copy, regenerate, Choose, Insert, the privacy sentence, a link to open the full comparison in the Model results tab, and access to the model pool editor). The Model results tab on the page keeps the full CandidateLedger.
13. Vocabulary: user-facing "annotation"/"Marginalia" become "note"/"notes" ("Add note", "Delete note", "{n} on this session"); the desk section "Alternatives" becomes "Rewrites". Keep "The Margin" as the desk's name. Update review-vocabulary.ts and tests accordingly; do not rename code identifiers or stored fields.
14. Keyboard (new, only when focus is not in a text field and no dialog is open): J / K next and previous turn; N note on the active turn; with a selection toolbar open, 1 / 2 / 3 pick the kind and R starts a rewrite; Escape keeps its current order of dismissals. Show them with kit `Kbd` in the hint bar. Keep Ctrl/Cmd+Enter to save a note.
R5 Phone
15. Below the desktop breakpoint: the header keeps back, title, the status control and Save (Export moves into an overflow); ONE tab row "Transcript / Notes / Rubric" (Rewrites and Model results reachable from an overflow or a fourth tab if it fits); a bottom bar with turn list button, previous / "Turn n of m" / next, and add-note; the note editor opens as a bottom sheet that leaves the quoted text visible.
Motion (see ENTER.index, ENTER.work, ENTER.note and the accept/apply handlers in ui-motion.js): rows and cards rise in on first paint; highlights draw left to right; note numbers pop; accepting a proposal turns its card solid, draws the highlight and bumps the Notes count; applying proposed scores fills them one after another; the tab underline slides (kit Tabs does this).
Icons: map the review vocabulary in review-icons.ts to Keating glyphs where one exists (pen, feather, ruler, compass, pattern, route, eye, lock, save, export, trash, edit, check, x, plus, refresh, copy, search, left, right, chev, more, layers, quote, bulb, alert, shield); keep Reicon for the rest. KeatingIcon call sites in your files may switch to KeatingGlyph.
Export: if web/src/components/export/ExportSheet.tsx exists when you finish, open it from Export with the review's inclusion summary before downloading; if it does not exist, keep the current direct download and say so.

Read-only and shared views (SharedTrajectoryWorkspace) must keep working and should pick up the new page styling without gaining any editing control.

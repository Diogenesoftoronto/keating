Why the base rate matters

Explanation

A test that is 99% accurate can still be wrong most of the time it says yes. What decides it is how common the condition is before anyone is tested.

With 10 cases in 100,000 people, the false alarms from the healthy 99,990 outnumber the true cases about a hundred to one.

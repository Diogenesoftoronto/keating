# Literature and quotation audit

Reviewed on 6 September 2026. This focused narrative review was conducted after the initial episode analysis. It revisited the previous Keating paper's bibliography and read primary research relevant to the observed lessons, creator use, and evaluation design. It was not a systematic review or a prespecified coding framework.

The previous report contains 13 cited references. Its systems results and model-to-model traces remain distinct from the creator-use evidence in this paper. The new bibliography contains 12 cited works: 11 external research papers and the preceding Keating technical report. Sources were selected for their connection to a particular interpretation, not copied wholesale from the previous bibliography.

## Interpretation changes from the review

- A correct guess is an instructional starting point: feedback and later answer production can develop it into retained knowledge.
- The creator's role is situated in autobiographical design, making sustained learning and application repair part of the case's contribution.
- The discussion compares Keating's observations with both positive structured-tutoring results and the divergence between assisted and unaided performance.
- Judge selection records the evaluator; checked examples and human ratings calibrate it.
- All design and population differences are collected in the manuscript's limitations section.

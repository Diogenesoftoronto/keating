Agent turn gaps to close (your specific ask)

Gap | Fix
No agent_turn_started | Add this — without it you can't measure latency or detect stuck turns
No model property | Add to agent_turn_completed and message_sent
No duration_ms | Measure from turn start to completion
No success/error flag | Can't distinguish clean turns from failed ones

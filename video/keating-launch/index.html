Recursion occurs when the definition of a concept or process depends on a simpler or previous version of itself. It is used in a variety of disciplines ranging from linguistics to logic, and most commonly in mathematics and computer science, where a function being defined is applied within its own definition. While this apparently defines an infinite number of instances, it is often done in such a way that no infinite loop or infinite chain of references can occur.

## Intent

A silent, fast feature tour for the Keating landing page. The product itself is
the proof: every beat uses current Keating pixels. Web, TUI, and CLI are moving
captures; model choice, Live, Coming Up, Courses, and Standard.site use clean
route or Storybook captures animated with restrained, seek-safe camera drift.

## Feature arc

1. Web classroom — ask and reconstruct in a streamed tutoring session.
2. Model choice — search, filter, and select by provider and capability.
3. Live media — full-duplex audio with native or sampled vision routing.
4. Coming Up / Anki — prioritize due work and carry decks in and out.
5. Courses — durable lessons, shared notes, peers, and teacher consent.
6. Standard.site — publish the Keating blog from an AT Protocol repository.
7. Collaborative TUI — continue the same learning thread in the terminal.
8. Artifact CLI — generate and inspect plans, maps, verifications, and traces.

## Customizations

- Keep each source leg independently addressable by a stable scene id.
- Use a continuous eight-step tracker so the breadth never reads as eight
  unrelated feature cards.
- Use the real Keating vocabulary and states. Do not invent users, customer
  quotes, performance figures, prices, or unsupported provider capabilities.
- Treat Web, TUI, and CLI footage as replaceable media slots; never restyle the
  recorded pixels.

## Notes

- Silent: no narration, music, or sound effects.
- Match the landing page's warm paper, dark ink, and Keating green.
- Preserve the captured product pixels and clearly identify their provenance.
- Hard cuts keep the pace legible; motion happens inside each four-second beat.
